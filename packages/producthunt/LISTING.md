## Description (≤ 260 chars)

Enter weight and height in kg/cm or lb/in and get your BMI, its WHO class and the healthy-weight range for your height, in Spanish or English. Rounds like the printed table, so no edge-case surprises. Also an Android app, API and libraries.

## First comment (maker)

Hi PH! Most BMI calculators are English-only, take only dots as decimals, and classify the raw number, so a 24.96 shows "normal" while the printed 25.0 says "overweight". Calculadora IMC rounds first, accepts "70,5" and "1,68", shows all eight WHO classes and tells you how many kilos you are from the healthy range. It is free, has no account and runs offline. The full calculator and methodology are on our Calculadora IMC site (https://imccalculadora.app/). Not medical advice. Feedback welcome!
